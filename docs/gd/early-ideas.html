Early ideas to optimize internal R&D processes at Genedata

General Ideas

Although my perspective of the internal R&D processes is limited at the moment, I tried to come up with some general ideas to optimize internal processes in the R&D department

An AI analyses the working patterns and habits of employees, as well as background information, and suggests collaborations based on probability of success.

Based on current list of tasks and past behavior, suggest ways of breaking down the task into multiple sub-tasks and order, to maximize efficiency.

An AI tool that generates reports automatically, and then allows changes in specific parts by voice commands, e.g., "Make the statements in this paragraph more convincing"

An audio and video analysis tool analyses experiment execution in real time, with the possibility to point out possible errors or answering questions about previous steps.

Leverage multimodal public data (news, videos, interviews, etc.) to predict possible trends in the pharma industry and suggest possible upgrades for products.

AI-generated music to improve focus and productivity, customized according to user's favorite music and/or mood.

A coding assistant with customizable voice that allows for conversations about code and knows when to stay quiet (Copilot sometimes interrupts the thinking process with comments that are not accurate).

A system for managing ideas from team members, who can submit ideas (anynimously also possible) and ideas are categorized automatically. Related references from previous attempts or related ideas from internal or public data could be added automatically.

LLM-based tool to guide new team members through a series of topics that will be fundamental for their success at the company. Answers questions based on official internal data (RAG).
