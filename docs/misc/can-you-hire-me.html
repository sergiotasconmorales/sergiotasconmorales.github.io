Hiring a third-country national in Switzerland

According to the SEM, non-EU/EFTA nationals must meet some admission criteria to access the Swiss employment market

  • Overall economic interest (Art. 18 and Art. 19 FNIA)
  • Quotas (Art. 20 FNIA)
  • Precedence (Art. 21 FNIA)
  • Obligation to give notice of vacant positions (Art. 21a FNIA)
  • Personal requirements (Art. 23 FNIA)
  • Accomodation (Art. 24 FNIA)

Exceptions to the admission critera

Article 30 of the FNIA establishes certain exceptions to the admision requirements:.

  • Management or specialist transfers
  • Internships and training and further education
  • Doctoral / post-doctoral students
  • Family members
  • Au pairs
  • Employment following study in Switzerland

Specifically, the last exception establishes

Foreign nationals can be admitted to work without having to provide evidence of their status under the precedence rule if there is a significant academic or economic interest in their employment. This rule applies only to degrees from recognised Swiss higher education institutions (universities and universities of applied sciences).
